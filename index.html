Football Speed Training for High School Players

Fourth & Fast Training teaches football players to start quicker, hit top speed sooner, and hold it late in the game. Our sessions are built around one idea: speed is a skill you can practice, not just a gift you were born with.

How our program works

Every athlete begins with a short timed assessment. Coach Dana Whitfield records a 10-yard split and a 40-yard time, then builds a four-week plan around the weakest part of the run. Most players focus on one of three areas.

  • The start. Learn a low, powerful drive phase in our 40-yard dash guide.
  • Daily practice. Follow our football speed drills for sprint mechanics and acceleration.
  • Quick direction changes. Build sharp cuts with our agility training plans.

About the coach

Coach Dana Whitfield is an invented former defensive back who spent twelve seasons coaching sprint technique to high school and college players. Dana's approach is simple: fix one thing at a time, measure it, and repeat.
