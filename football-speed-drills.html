Football Speed Drills for Beginners

You do not need a gym or special equipment. These six drills fit in a 30-minute practice on any field, and they teach the mechanics behind fast running.

Before you start

Jog for five minutes and do dynamic stretches such as leg swings and walking lunges. Rest fully between sprints, because speed work done tired only practices slow running.

The six drills

DrillSets and repsCoaching cue
Wall drive3 sets of 5 per legStraight line from head to heel, drive the knee up
A-skip3 x 15 yardsKnee up, toe up, strike under the hips
High-knee march3 x 15 yardsTall posture, fast arms
Falling start4 x 10 yardsLean forward until you must step
Hill sprint5 x 20 yards, walk backPush the ground away, stay low
Flying 204 x 20 yards after a 15-yard build-upRelax your face and hands

How often to train

Two or three speed sessions per week is plenty for most high school players. Keep each session short and stop when your form slips. Quality repetitions matter more than a long workout.

Tip: Film yourself from the side on your phone. Check that your head stays still and your arms drive straight forward and back.
