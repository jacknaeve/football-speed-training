Football Agility Training: Cut Faster

Agility is the ability to slow down, change direction, and speed up again without losing balance. It decides who wins on a route, a block, or a tackle.

What good cutting looks like

Lower your hips, plant your outside foot, and push off hard in the new direction. Your knee should stay over your toes. Keep your chest up and your eyes on where you are going.

Build the base first

Strong hips, ankles, and core protect you when you cut. Add bodyweight squats, single-leg balance holds, and planks twice a week. Work at a pace that keeps your form clean.
